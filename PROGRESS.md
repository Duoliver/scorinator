- **2026-09-25 (Engine — extreme scores in big OVR mismatches. Open issue, no task yet):** The user read the study below and judged it not a big problem for now. So no task exists, and a session must not fix it without asking. **Study:** the real engine, 300,000 matches per case, home advantage on at 5%. Real-football figures are approximate and from memory. (1) In aggregate, extreme scores are rare. A same-tier league (OVR 60-69) gives 2-1 13.8%, 6-0 0.14%, draws 28.1%, margin of 4+ goals 3.8%. A league of all tiers S to F gives 2-1 10.9%, 6-0 0.82%, draws 23.4%, margin of 4+ goals 9.6%, a team scoring 6+ goals 2.8%. Real football is about 2-3% for a margin of 4+ goals. (2) In big mismatches, extreme scores are common. Favorite at home, gap 25 OVR (80 v 55): 2-1 7.95%, 6-0 0.73%, margin of 4+ goals 9.7%. Gap 55 (95 v 40): 2-1 3.0%, 6-0 3.7%, margin of 4+ goals 27.7%. Gap 69 (99 v 30): 2-1 2.7%, 6-0 4.9%, margin of 4+ goals 34.0%. From a gap of about 55, 6-0 is as common as 2-1. (3) The favorite scoring 11+ goals: 1 in 6,700 matches at 85 v 50, 1 in 910 at 95 v 35, 1 in 520 at 99 v 30. **Cause:** each OVR point adds 1.5% to expected goals, linearly (`DIFF_WEIGHT`), so at a gap of 60 the favorite's factor is about 1.9 and the underdog's hits its floor. The shared elasticity roll (uniform 0.4 to 1.6) then scales both. At the top of the roll the favorite's expected goals reach about 4.3, and the underdog's stay near 0.2. The same elasticity range gives low-scoring matches too, which is probably why draws are 28% instead of about 25%. **User stance:** attenuate the common extremes (4 to 6 goals), but keep a real chance of 11-0 or more for an A or S team against an F team. **Design note for a future session:** lowering `DIFF_WEIGHT`, saturating the difference factor, or narrowing elasticity would each cut the rare tail along with the common extremes. Keeping a rare tail while cutting the common extremes probably needs a separate mechanism for the tail. This is untested. **Constraints:** the OVR-difference vs absolute-OVR weighting is a `CLAUDE.md` open item, and a change to the difference factor touches it, so propose an approach and get a decision first. One OVR point is worth 1.5% of goals today, so a change to that scale moves the meaning of the Task 32 values. Do this before the Task 32 tuning, or retune Task 32 after. The study scripts are not in the repo.
