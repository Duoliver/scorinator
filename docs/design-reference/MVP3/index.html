Each page has a "Demo controls" box at the bottom right. It is not part of the app. A link with #create, #taken, #delete (list), or #add, #edit, #finish (view) opens that dialog.

Points to review

  1. The menu. "Seasons" sits between "Leagues" and "Teams".
  2. The Season card. The year is a dark badge next to the name. A "Running" or "Finished" badge, the counts, "Open Season", and a ⋮ menu with "Rename..." and "Delete...".
  3. The year field. A four-digit year, such as 2026. A split year, such as 2025/26, is not possible in the demo.
  4. Rename and delete. Only in the ⋮ menu of the card, not in the Season view.
  5. The Season view. A "Leagues" section with the league cards above the tabs. "+ New league" is in that section.
  6. Finish and reopen. A button in the header. "Finish Season" asks for a confirm. "Reopen Season" does not. A finished Season shows a notice, and its buttons are disabled.
  7. The roster. Columns: colour, team and Location, trigram, Tier, OVR, leagues. A team in no league shows "Rests: in no league".
  8. A name clash. "Ashfield Rovers (Northtown)" shows its Location in the name, and again next to it.
  9. The add dialog. The "Add teams" style. It lists only the teams that are not in the Season. A hint shows the new name of a clashing team.
  10. The edit drawer. "Edit Season team", with a note that names the leagues of the team. The fields of the wizard Edit drawer: name, slug, Tier, OVR, trigram, colour.
